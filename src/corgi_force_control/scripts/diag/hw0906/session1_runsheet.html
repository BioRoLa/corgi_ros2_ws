Code fact
Found at the bench 2026-09-02: there is no standing lean. gamma_openloop() is gated on yaw_ref_set_ — the "template is actually running" flag (gslip_pronk.cpp:2774 inside gamma_openloop(), deliberate: a held offset during the settle would twist the legs against the ground). A lean launch stands up perfectly straight and stays straight; the camber engages only after the trigger — and not immediately: yaw_ref_set_ flips at the end of the 2500-tick settle, so the ACKER CAMBER set banner and the 500 ms lean ramp arrive ~2.5 s after you trigger. Don't abort a lean cell for a missing banner before then.
